Lee Kuan Yew was a man with determination, discipline and the will to change Singapore.

Lee Kuan Yew was determined to improve the lives of Singaporeans. He fought for merger with Malaysia, but after the merger fell apart he managed to lead Singapore to even greater heights, first developing Singapore's industrial manufacturing capacity, attracting foreign invvestors and Multi-National Corporations. As the industrial field developed, Lee Kuan Yew had to make many important choices, implementing National Service after the British pulled its troops from Singapore.

Lee Kuan Yew overcame many challenges, such as Singapore's small size, lack of a hinterland and lack of natural resources, investing in education, producing a top notch education system. He managed to leverage Singapore's greatest resource - Human Resource and turned Singapore's greatest sector of jobs from industrial manufacturing to skill-based jobs and brought Singapore to a period of prosperity.

Personally, I think he has done a lot for Singapore. He had brought Singapore from the third world to the first world despite all odds, turning Singapore into the most propsperous country in Southeast Asia and beyond. The education system I am going through today is only possible as Lee Kuan Yew improved the lives of so many Singaporeans.
